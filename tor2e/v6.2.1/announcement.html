TOR2E v6.2.1

v6.2.1 Release date · 2026-08-30 · Foundry VTT 14

TOR2E v6.2.1 is a focused patch release for Foundry VTT 14, strengthening combat handling, Actor data integrity, localization and sheet polish.

The most important changes are:

  • ⚔️ more reliable combat context and stance handling, including tokens involved in multiple encounters;
  • ◆ safer Actor sheet submissions, preventing Active Effect contributions from leaking into stored source values;
  • 💾 regression protection for Actor image persistence and combat initialization;
  • 🌍 improved localization, including Spanish updates, corrected language names and localized weapon groups;
  • 🖥️ further Character, Adversary and Lore sheet polish, including clearer TOR2E resource terminology;
  • 🧪 stronger E2E fixtures and CSS guardrails to reduce future regressions.

This patch continues the reliability work introduced in v6.2.0: fewer hidden data side effects, better alignment with Foundry VTT 14 lifecycle behavior, and stronger automated coverage of table-critical workflows.
